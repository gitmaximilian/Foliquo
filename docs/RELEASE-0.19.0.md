# Foliquo Community v0.19.0

Foliquo is simpler to use: it now opens in a simple mode, "My style" works in one step, and you choose how the app looks. It also has a new logo.

## Simple and professional modes

- Foliquo now starts in simple mode. The menu shows Translate, Archive and My style, plus "Ask Foli" and Settings.
- Rules, terms, dossiers, jobs and the guide are professional tools. Turn them on in **Settings → Professional tools**. Nothing was removed; they are only hidden.
- Each professional tool has its own page in the menu. The style area no longer combines examples, terms, rules and dossiers on one page.
- If your workspace already has rules, terms or dossiers, it opens with the professional tools turned on.

## My style in one step

- Add a translation you finished. Foliquo lists what it learned, such as the date format, how family names are written or the alignment of headings.
- Select **Apply** to keep everything, or remove single entries with ✕ first (**Apply 2 of 3**). **Discard** keeps nothing. Applying also turns the example on, so there is no second step.
- "What Foliquo already follows" groups your habits by document type. One switch turns a group on or off.
- The doubled "Detected style and analysis" section in older examples is fixed. German labels now say "Beispiel" instead of "Muster".

## Always write it this way

- When you correct a short passage in the result, such as a name, Foliquo asks once: **Always write it this way** or **Only here**.
- "Always" saves your spelling as a rule for future translations into the same language. You find it under My style and can switch it off there.

## At most three questions

- In simple mode, Foliquo asks at most three questions per job, starting with unreadable places.
- Further unclear places are not guessed. They get an "illegible" note in the translation, and names use the suggested or usual spelling. Both are marked as decided without asking, so they stand out in the review.
- Professional mode keeps asking every question, as before.

## Designs

- Choose a design in **Settings → Design** or when you first start: Automatic, Light, Dark or the new **Glass**.
- Glass is a translucent design with soft colour fields behind frosted surfaces. It follows your system's light or dark setting. Documents stay on white paper. Systems that reduce transparency get solid surfaces.
- The dark mode button in the menu moved into this choice.

## New logo

- The new mark shows two overlapping pages: the original and its translation. It is used in the app, favicons, the demo and the desktop apps.
- All brand files are generated from one source by `scripts/build-brand.mjs`.

## A calmer interface

- Cooler neutral background, softer edges and light elevation instead of outlines.
- The translate page no longer shows "Rules · 0 effective · scope pending". When your style applies, it says so in a sentence.

## Practical limits

Model output, OCR, notes and reconstructed layouts still require review. Foliquo never sets a seal or signature and does not certify.
