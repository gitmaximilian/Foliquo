# Foliquo Community v0.20.0

Every point from the 0.19 design review is done: one column to translate, a review page that leads to the download, settings in three sections and calmer, consistent controls.

## Translate

- One column. The languages read as a sentence at the top of the card ("From Automatic to German"), the drop area fills it, and "More options" folds the job settings away.
- In simple mode, "More options" keeps only the AI connection, extra details and "Use my style". Templates, references and dossiers stay in professional mode.
- "Your documents" lists recent jobs as one row each. "Show all" opens the archive in simple mode.

## Review

- The original and the translation come first. Both open fitted to their width.
- Below them, the check list reads like a to-do list: tick, label, comparison, and "Open check" as a quiet link. Empty sections and the professional rule details are hidden in simple mode.
- Beside the list, one column holds the certification and the download. The certification shows one line ("Paper with seal · Original, attached") with "Change"; the full form opens when you change it or when your profile still needs setting up.
- One count of open checks. "All other names, dates and numbers checked against the original" says what that check covers.
- A compact step indicator, "Edit text" in the header and "Tools" instead of "Review & tools". On a phone the document comes first.

## Questions

- The question is the heading, its location a quiet line below. "View the original" and "Illegible" are calm buttons. The step indicator matches the review page.

## Settings

- Three sections: General (design, professional tools, certification), AI (connection and usage) and Data & updates.
- The sidebar opens General; every "Set up AI" link opens AI directly.
- In simple mode, usage is one summary line; filters and single model calls are in professional mode. Connection diagnostics sit inside "Manage connection".

## Everywhere

- German uses "Version" throughout instead of mixing "Fassung" and "Version".
- One button system: a filled main action, quiet secondary buttons with a hairline, links in the accent colour.
- Frames only around things you act on; groups inside dialogs are separated by space.
- My style: the provider note sits in the drop area, examples are compact rows, habits are one list. Foli: the chat picker appears from the second chat, examples are chips above the message field. The archive has one search with "Also search the text". The footer shows on the start page only.
- Checked in German, English and Arabic; light, dark and Glass; from 360 to 1920 pixels wide, in simple and professional mode.

## Practical limits

Model output, OCR, notes and reconstructed layouts still require review. Foliquo never sets a seal or signature and does not certify.
