# Foliquo Community v0.17.0

This version prepares certified translations after the ÖVGD guideline (12/2024). After a one-time setup, the header, certification clause, place and date are placed automatically. Foliquo never sets a seal or signature and does not certify; the certified translator reviews, seals and signs.

## Certification

- One-time certification profile under Settings → Certification: place, your listed languages, bilingual clause, usual source form and usual route.
- The review page shows a certification panel with the header and clause exactly as exported. With a profile the clause is on by default. Per document you can change the source form (attached or presented original, attached copy, attached certified copy), mark an excerpt and choose paper or electronic signature.
- The clause uses the § 190 AußStrG wording and follows the actual end of the text after a rule. It carries place and production date, adds a labelled English or Arabic version where one of the languages is English or Arabic, and leaves 5 cm for seal and signature.
- Headers read "Beglaubigte Übersetzung aus der … Sprache in die … Sprache" ("auszugsweise" for excerpts) with a second line in the other language for English, Arabic, French, Spanish, Italian and Turkish. German header wording changed from "aus dem Arabischen ins Deutsche" to the guideline's form.
- Before export, Foliquo warns about a missing place, languages not in your profile, relay pairs without German, runs of empty lines, signatures missing from the text and plain copies.
- After download, a checklist lists the steps for the paper route (permanent connection, seal, corrections, hand-over), the electronic route (sign the exported PDF with ID Austria outside Foliquo; valid only electronically) and use abroad (intermediate certification, apostille or BMEIA and foreign mission). This is information, not legal advice.

## Other changes

- After you open a finished translation for review, the studio returns to the upload for the next document; the finished job stays on the desk.
- A workspace that cannot be opened now names the reason: wrong key or damaged file, unreadable content, invalid structure, or a workspace saved by a newer Foliquo. A newer workspace is never replaced by an older recovery point. The desktop window shows the service's own reason instead of only the log file name.

If an older desktop app reports that your workspace "could not be decrypted", update the desktop app. The workspace was most likely saved by a newer Foliquo.

## Practical limits

Foliquo checks wording and placement. It cannot check whether you are listed for a language, whether your seal meets the requirements, or how the documents are physically connected. Requirements of receiving authorities differ; confirm them first. Model output, OCR, notes and reconstructed layouts require review.
