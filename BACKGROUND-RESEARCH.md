# Background research

Foliquo is intended to help a translator prepare draft translations of official documents for Austrian use. It is not a substitute for an authorized court-certified translator. A target authority may also require legalization or apostille steps that are separate from translation.

The interface should show the intended language pair and preserve original pages alongside the translated draft. Dates use `DD.MM.YYYY` when supported by clear source information. Arabic names may need a user-confirmed Latin spelling; uncertain or unreadable values should be asked about or marked unreadable, never guessed.

Users should verify the current requirements with the receiving authority and check the cited primary sources in the in-app guide before relying on a translation workflow.
